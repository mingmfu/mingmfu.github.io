Dr. Ming Fu has served as Chief Expert of Huawei Fields Lab at Huawei Technologies since July 2026 and is based in Shanghai, China. He previously served as Director of Huawei Fields Lab. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Science and Technology of China in 2010, conducted visiting research at Yale University, and later served as an associate professor at USTC.

His research covers operating systems, concurrency, weak memory models, formal verification of systems software, and parallel graphics rendering. His verification work includes program logics and automated verification. His recent graphics work includes D-VSync (ASPLOS 2025), which decouples rendering from display refresh, and Spars (OSDI 2025), which parallelizes the OS rendering service through out-of-order execution and in-order commit. From 2019 to 2023, he was the founding director of Huawei Dresden Research Center and led research on concurrent-program verification, including VSync/libvsync.

His work has appeared at POPL, CAV, SOSP, OSDI, ASPLOS, and USENIX ATC. VSync received an ASPLOS 2021 Distinguished Paper Award, and jwmalloc was nominated for an OSDI 2026 Best Paper Award.
